Background

The Pivot That Changed Everything

For over twenty years I ran professional kitchens as an executive chef and restaurant owner. That world runs on structured triage, managing cascading failures in real time, and building teams that perform under pressure. Those are exactly the skills that make a good SOC analyst.

After completing a B.S. in Computer Information Systems with a cybersecurity concentration from the University of Akron, I made the full transition into security operations. I have worked as a SOC analyst at Sam Tech / Posterity Cyber Solutions, monitored 500+ endpoints in a 24/7 environment, and completed ISSO-adjacent contract work supporting the Library of Congress.

I am currently completing a B.S. in Cybersecurity and Information Assurance at Western Governors University and hold CompTIA Security+. My target is the federal and defense space in the DC/MD/VA corridor, with a long-term goal on red team and offensive security operations.

Every tool on this page I have built, broken, and fixed myself. None of this is theoretical.
